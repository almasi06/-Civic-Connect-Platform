# CivicConnect – Community Service Request Management Platform  

## 📌 Project Overview  
Civic Connect is a controlled digital platform designed to manage community service requests such as facility faults, maintenance issues, IT support, security concerns, and other operational needs. The project consolidates prior programming, database, and web development knowledge into a full software engineering lifecycle, emphasizing **traceability, accountability, and quality evidence** rather than just working code.  

---

## Project Purpose  
The goal is to demonstrate competence in engineering, controlling, justifying, verifying, and defending a software product across its lifecycle.  

Key objectives include:  
- Stakeholder analysis and scope definition.  
- Requirements engineering with traceability and acceptance criteria.  
- Architecture and design decisions supported by ADRs.  
- Risk management and controlled change processes.  
- Quality assurance through measurable evidence.  
- Secure collaboration using GitHub governance and CI/CD pipelines.  
- Responsible AI usage with verification and accountability.  

---

## Minimum Business Capabilities  

### Requester  
- Submit new service requests.  
- Categorize requests using controlled categories.  
- Track request status and history.  
- Receive feedback on updates and resolutions.  

### Staff  
- View, filter, and search relevant requests.  
- Assign or accept responsibility.  
- Update status through controlled transitions.  
- Record actions, comments, and resolutions.  

### Management  
- Monitor open, overdue, resolved, and closed requests.  
- Access categorized and status-based reporting.  
- Support accountability and service-performance analysis.  

---

## Project Constraints  
- **Team Size:** Exactly 3 students.  
- **Schedule:** 4 milestones within SEN381 delivery period.  
- **Cost:** Prefer free/low-cost services; justify limitations.  
- **Scope:** Controlled baseline; no uncontrolled scope creep.  
- **Quality:** Defined NFRs with measurable evidence.  
- **Security:** Lifecycle-wide responsibility.  
- **Technology:** No prescribed stack; decisions must be justified.  

---

## Milestones  
1. **Engineering Foundation & Requirements Baseline**  
2. **Architecture, Design & Engineering Decisions**  
3. **Controlled Construction, Integration, Quality & Release Readiness**  
4. **Final Product, Project Success & Engineering Defence**  

Each milestone combines **team artefacts** with **individual accountability** through presentations and engineering defence.  

---

## Repository Standards  
- **Main branch protected** – no direct commits.  
- **Pull Requests required** – minimum of 2 approvals.  
- **Meaningful reviews** – correctness, maintainability, security, and traceability.  
- **Progressive history** – authentic commits, issues, and documentation.
