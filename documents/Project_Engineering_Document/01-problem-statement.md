## Problem and Business Need

Civic Connect addresses the lack of transparency in municipal service delivery and citizen to government communication. The core problem centres on inefficient reporting channels for public infrastructure issues (e.g. water leaks, potholes, power outages etc.) and the absence of a unified tracking mechanism for municipal updates. Below are the intended improvements before completion:

**Intended Business Value:** Streamlines municipal operations by automating intake routing, reducing redundant call centre volumes and providing data-driven visibility into recurring neighbourhood infrastructure failures.

**Intended Stakeholder Value:** Empower citizens and local businesses with transparent, real-time tracking of reported incidents, while giving municipal administrators structured data to prioritize resource deployment.

**Project Engineering Document (PED) Integration:** This problem definition directly anchors the project engineering document (PED v1.0), ensuring that subsequent functional and non-functional requirements trace back to solving specific inefficiencies identified in municipal workflows.
