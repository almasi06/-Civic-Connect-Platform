# Stakeholder Analysis
Effective system design requires mapping out the stakeholders who interact with Civic Connect, their core interests and the inherent conflicts that emerge between them.

| Stakeholders | Core Needs | Interest & Influence | Potential Conflicts & Competing Expectations |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Citizens/Residents** | Fast issue reporting, transparent progress updates, easy-to-use web interface | High interest and Low to moderate influence | Want immediate resolution and constant updates, which conflict with municipal resource constraints and budget limits. |
| **Municipal Field Workers and Technicians** | Clear work orders, GPS locations, offline capability, and status update tools | Moderate interest and influence | Need granular, structural data before travelling to a site, which can conflict with citizens submitting vague or incomplete reports. |
| **Municipal Administrators** | Analytics dashboards, performance reporting, resource allocation tools. | High interest and influence | Require strict accountability and compliance reporting. |
